Privacy Policy

Welcome to addisoncountyeagles.org. Addison County Eagles respects your privacy and wants visitors to understand how information may be collected and used when they visit this website or contact the organization.

Personally Identifiable Information

Personally identifiable information means information that can identify, contact, or locate a person. This may include a name, mailing address, phone number, email address, or other information a visitor chooses to provide through a form, email, or direct communication.

Information We May Collect

We may collect basic contact information when a visitor submits a form, asks a question, requests information, or contacts Addison County Eagles. This may include name, email address, phone number, and the contents of a message.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, provide requested information, improve the website, support lodge communications, and manage ordinary website operations. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar browser technologies to support basic site functionality, remember preferences, understand how visitors use the site, or protect the website. Visitors can adjust browser settings to refuse cookies, though some features may not work as intended.

Service Providers

Website hosts, analytics tools, form services, email providers, or other service providers may process limited information needed to operate this website. Their use of information is governed by their own policies and agreements.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. No website, email, or electronic storage system can be guaranteed completely secure, so visitors should avoid sending sensitive information through general website forms.

Corrections and Opt Out Requests

Visitors may ask to update, correct, or remove personal information by contacting Addison County Eagles at addisoncountyeagles@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian believes a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Legal Compliance

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, subpoena, or a lawful request from public authorities, or when reasonably necessary to protect the safety of visitors, members, or the organization.

Links

This website may eventually include links to other websites. Once a visitor leaves addisoncountyeagles.org, the privacy practices of the other website apply.

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy by posting a revised version on this page.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to Addison County Eagles at addisoncountyeagles@gmail.com, by phone at 802.877.2055, or by mail at 67 New Haven Rd, Vergennes VT 05491.
