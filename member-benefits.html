Member Benefits

The Benefits Of Being An Eagle

The perks of being an Eagle member are countless. From lifelong friendships to care for your family, Eagles are always ready to lend a helping hand and be a guiding influence in the lives of every member. A renewed focus on membership growth and retention continues to strengthen the slate of benefits available to members.

★

Memorial Foundation

Since World War II, Eagles have offered protection for the families of members through the Memorial Foundation. If a member dies while performing their job or serving their country, their children may be eligible to receive educational grants, medical benefits, dental benefits, and more.

★

Aerie Amenities

Members have access to more than 1,500 Aeries across the United States and Canada. Local Aeries can offer discounted food and drinks, billiards, darts, dancing, karaoke, card games, sporting events, banquets, and parties.

★

Eagle Village

Eagle Village is a living community in Bradenton, Florida, located minutes from the Gulf of Mexico. The 26-acre complex features single-bedroom units, a library, recreation center, pool, gym, shuffleboard, and a three-acre lake.

Affinity Programs

FOE members have access to a broad collection of member programs and discounts. These include travel savings, insurance options, hearing benefits, prescription savings, security services, mortgage programs, Medicare support, supplemental insurance, and other partner offers.

Members looking to sign up for discounts should use the official Members-Only benefits area or contact the Addison County Eagles for local guidance.

Farmers Insurance

Information for members on coverage options including home, auto, business, life, and recreational insurance.

Life Line Screening

Discounted health screenings that help identify hidden risks for stroke and cardiovascular disease.

Travel Savings Center

Member savings on car rentals, hotels, ticketing, condos, cruises, guided tours, and group travel packages.

American Hearing Benefits

Hearing consultations, nationwide hearing locations, hearing-aid discounts, trial periods, and warranty benefits.

LMT Club

Exclusive access to travel rates for hotels, flights, rental cars, cruises, and vacation homes.

Office Depot & OfficeMax

Discounts on office supplies, printing, cleaning supplies, furniture, and online delivery options.

Mutual Of Omaha

Guaranteed acceptance whole life insurance options for eligible members.

Emergency Assistance Plus

Travel and medical emergency assistance such as evacuation, transportation home, and personal security services.

Norton 360 With LifeLock

Online protection, VPN privacy, device security, and identity theft protection discounts.

Neptune Society

Pre-arranged plan options, cremation services, and member discounts through Neptune Society.

Orlando Vacation Discounts

Savings on theme park tickets, vacation packages, Orlando hotels, and vacation homes.
